Opportunity Detection Method

How the opportunity score is calculated.

01
Current Business Value — 60% Measures the relative current value generated by each selected dimension.
02
Positive Growth — 40% When a date column is available, positive movement between periods contributes to the opportunity score.
03
Opportunity Classification Scores are classified into High, Medium, and Low opportunity levels using the analysis thresholds.
Score Formula
Opportunity Score = 60% Value + 40% Growth
