How to interpret Customer Intelligence

Smart BI uses only the selected Customers dataset for this page. The system calculates customer behaviour from purchase date, customer identifier and spending. RFM combines Recency, Frequency and Monetary value to classify customers into High Value, Loyal, New, At Risk, Developing, Low Value and Inactive groups. The selected date range controls period-based KPIs, while lifetime customer metrics continue to use the complete historical customer dataset so that customer lifetime value and first-purchase behaviour are not incorrectly reset by the filter.
